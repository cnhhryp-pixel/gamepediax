Baldur's Gate 3 Rest and Resources Reference

A Baldur's Gate 3 rest and resources reference for deciding when to conserve limited abilities and when to reset the party.

Resource management is not about saving everything forever. It is about spending enough power to keep progress efficient without entering the next fight depleted.

Use renewable options for routine encounters

Basic attacks and reusable abilities can handle easier situations while limited resources remain available.

Spend strong abilities when they meaningfully change the outcome

If a powerful action prevents major damage or ends a dangerous fight quickly, the cost may be justified.

Rest when key roles can no longer function well

If several party members have lost the resources that define their role, continuing may create more risk than value.

Review what actually ran out

After a difficult section, identify the resource that became the bottleneck and prepare around it next time.
