Baldur's Gate 3 Inventory and Consumables Reference

A Baldur's Gate 3 inventory and consumables reference for keeping useful items visible and reducing clutter.

Inventory organization should make decisions faster. The best system is one where useful consumables and active equipment are easy to find during play.

Keep combat consumables visible

Potions, scrolls and situational items are more valuable when they can be found quickly.

Separate active gear from experiments

Move unused alternatives out of the main decision space until you are ready to compare them.

Remove obvious clutter regularly

Selling or moving low-value items prevents the inventory from becoming a search problem.

Distribute items by character role

Put useful items on the characters most likely to use them so access is faster during combat.
