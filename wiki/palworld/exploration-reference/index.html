Palworld Exploration Reference

A Palworld exploration reference for trip objectives, supplies, team coverage and deciding when to return.

Exploration becomes more productive when every trip has one main reason behind it.

Choose one primary objective

A clear target makes route, inventory and team decisions easier.

Prepare enough for the distance

Bring enough food and essentials without filling the inventory with unrelated items.

Leave room for rewards

A full inventory before departure guarantees awkward decisions later.

Return when the objective is complete

Convert the trip into upgrades instead of stretching every journey until it becomes risky.
