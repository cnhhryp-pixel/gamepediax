Elden Ring Build Basics Reference

A spoiler-light Elden Ring build basics reference for choosing a weapon, supporting survivability and avoiding scattered stat investment.

A build is easier to understand when it starts from the way you like to fight. Early progression should support a clear core style rather than several unrelated ideas.

Start with a weapon or style you understand

A comfortable moveset gives you a stable foundation for learning enemy timing and positioning.

Survivability buys learning time

Enough durability to survive mistakes makes each encounter more informative and less binary.

Keep stat investment coherent

Meet useful requirements and reinforce the main approach before spreading levels across several damage paths.

Adapt when new information changes the plan

A build should evolve when a new weapon or mechanic genuinely fits your play style better.
