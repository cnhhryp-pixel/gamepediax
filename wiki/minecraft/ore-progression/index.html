Minecraft Ore Progression Reference

A practical Minecraft ore progression reference for deciding what to mine, what to save and how early resources support survival progression.

Mining is easiest to manage when each resource has a job. Early ores should improve the tools, protection and utility that make the next mining trip safer and more productive.

Iron and early utility

Iron usually marks the shift from basic survival into more reliable tools, protection and utility. Spend it on upgrades that remove repeated bottlenecks instead of crafting everything immediately.

Save rare resources for clear upgrades

When a resource is harder to replace, wait until you know which upgrade will materially improve progression. Avoid spending rare materials just because a recipe is available.

Separate mining stock from daily supplies

Keep ores and processed materials organized so you can see what is abundant and what still limits the next upgrade.

Use each mining trip to strengthen the next

The best mining loop is simple: gather, return, process, upgrade, reorganize and leave with a clearer objective.
