No Man's Sky Exploration Systems Reference

A No Man's Sky exploration systems reference for trip objectives, travel readiness, inventory space and return decisions.

Exploration becomes more productive when every trip has a reason behind it, even if that reason is simply discovery.

Give the trip one main objective

A clear reason makes route, inventory and upgrade decisions easier.

Check travel readiness before leaving

Basic movement and survival supplies should not become emergency problems mid-trip.

Leave room for discoveries

A full inventory limits the value of unexpected finds.

Return when the trip has produced a clear gain

Convert discoveries into upgrades, credits or storage before wandering indefinitely.
