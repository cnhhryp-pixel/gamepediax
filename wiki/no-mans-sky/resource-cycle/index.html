No Man's Sky Resource Cycle Reference

A No Man's Sky resource cycle reference for keeping essential materials stable and gathering with purpose.

A stable resource cycle removes repeated emergency gathering and gives exploration more freedom.

Keep core resources above emergency level

A small reserve prevents travel or survival from being interrupted at the worst moment.

Gather toward a specific goal

Collect materials tied to an upgrade, construction or trade decision rather than filling inventory randomly.

Process and organize quickly

Convert raw materials into useful forms or storage before the next trip.

Add complexity after the core loop is stable

New production goals are easier to manage once basic travel and survival needs are reliable.
