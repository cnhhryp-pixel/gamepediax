Cyberpunk 2077 Mission Preparation Reference

A Cyberpunk 2077 mission preparation reference for loadout, healing, cyberware synergy and inventory space.

A short preparation routine can remove many avoidable problems before a mission begins.

Check the primary loadout

Make sure the weapons you intend to use fit the build and are ready for the mission.

Check recovery options

Do not discover during a difficult encounter that healing or defensive options are missing.

Check cyberware synergy

The setup should reinforce one coherent play style rather than several unrelated ideas.

Leave room for rewards

Inventory space makes post-mission comparison and cleanup much easier.
