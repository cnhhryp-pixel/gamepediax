Subnautica Depth and Oxygen Reference

A Subnautica depth and oxygen reference for understanding safe exploration range, navigation and return planning.

Depth progression works best when oxygen margin and navigation improve together.

Create breathing margin

More oxygen or better mobility gives you time to observe and make decisions instead of rushing.

Keep the route understandable

Markers and landmarks make deeper exploration safer and easier to repeat.

Check support systems

Power, repairs and storage can become the new bottleneck even after depth capability improves.

Use first trips for reconnaissance

Learn routes and hazards before turning deeper areas into resource destinations.
