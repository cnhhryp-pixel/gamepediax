Subnautica Inventory Reference

A Subnautica inventory reference for expedition loadouts, storage categories and cleaner resource runs.

Inventory works best when tools and survival supplies stay predictable and collected materials are treated as temporary cargo.

Keep an expedition loadout

Tools, food, water and emergency supplies should occupy predictable space.

Use broad storage categories

Simple material groups are easier to maintain than an overly detailed system.

Leave empty slots before leaving

Inventory capacity is part of expedition preparation.

Reset after every trip

Unload before starting another objective so old cargo does not reduce the next run.
