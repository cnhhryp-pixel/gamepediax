Subnautica Base and Power Reference

A Subnautica base and power reference for choosing a practical first habitat, organizing storage and keeping essential systems reliable.

A base should reduce travel and inventory friction. Reliability matters more than size during early progression.

Choose a location for convenience

A recognizable site near useful resources is often more valuable than a perfect scenic location.

Solve storage before decoration

Storage makes every expedition easier by reducing clutter and repeated trips.

Keep power demand predictable

Add systems gradually so the base remains reliable instead of becoming dependent on a fragile setup.

Expand when a new system needs room

Vehicles, scanning and additional crafting are good reasons to grow the habitat.
