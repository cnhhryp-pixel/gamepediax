Best Early Cyberware in Cyberpunk 2077: How to Prioritize

A Cyberpunk 2077 early-cyberware guide focused on choosing upgrades that reinforce survivability, mobility or a clear combat loop.

The best early cyberware is not one universal list. Prioritize pieces that reinforce the way you already fight and remove a problem you feel repeatedly.

Prioritize survivability when pressure ends fights early

Defensive value matters when the build cannot stay active long enough to use its damage.

Prioritize mobility when positioning is the bottleneck

Movement improvements can make both offense and defense more consistent.

Choose pieces that work together

Several upgrades supporting one loop usually create more value than unrelated improvements.

Reassess after major build changes

When the core weapon or combat style changes, cyberware priorities may change with it.
