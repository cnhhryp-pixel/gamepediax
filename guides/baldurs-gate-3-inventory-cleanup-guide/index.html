Baldur's Gate 3 Inventory Cleanup Guide

A practical Baldur's Gate 3 inventory cleanup guide for consumables, equipment, clutter and distributing useful items by role.

Inventory should help decisions, not hide them. A simple cleanup routine makes useful items easier to find during combat and exploration.

Keep consumables easy to find

Potions, scrolls and situational tools are more valuable when they are visible at the moment you need them.

Separate active gear from experiments

Move unused alternatives away from the main loadout until you are ready to compare them.

Remove obvious clutter regularly

Selling or moving low-value items keeps later decisions fast.

Distribute items by likely user

Put items on the characters most likely to use them so access is faster in combat.
