No Man's Sky Planet Exploration Checklist

A No Man's Sky planet exploration checklist covering travel resources, inventory space, scanning, hazard readiness and a clear trip objective.

Exploration is more productive when the trip has a purpose and enough free inventory to make discoveries worth keeping.

Choose one main objective

Decide whether the trip is for resources, scanning, upgrades or general discovery.

Check travel resources

Basic movement should not become an emergency halfway through the trip.

Leave inventory space

A full inventory reduces the value of anything useful you discover.

Return after a clear gain

Convert discoveries into upgrades, storage or trade before wandering indefinitely.

Planet exploration readiness table

AreaReady when…What it prevents

InventoryYou have room for useful findsDiscard decisions before the trip produces value

Use an explore–convert–reset loop

Explore until the trip produces one clear gain, then return and convert that gain into storage, upgrades or trade. Reset the inventory before leaving again. This keeps exploration flexible without letting each trip dissolve into permanent clutter.

No Man's Sky planet exploration FAQ

Should every planet trip have a strict objective?

No. General discovery is a valid objective, but it still helps to know what will make the trip feel complete.

How much inventory space should I leave?

Enough that useful discoveries can be kept without immediately discarding other items.

When should I return instead of exploring longer?

Return after a clear gain when continuing mainly adds risk, clutter or repeated travel.
