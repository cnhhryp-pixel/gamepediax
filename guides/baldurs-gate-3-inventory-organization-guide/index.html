Baldur's Gate 3 Inventory Organization Guide

A simple Baldur's Gate 3 inventory organization guide for consumables, equipment, quest items and reducing clutter between encounters.

Inventory organization should make useful items easier to find during decisions, not create a sorting project after every pickup.

1. Keep consumables easy to identify

Group potions, scrolls and other combat-use items so they are visible when a fight becomes difficult.

2. Separate active gear from experiments

Keep current equipment clear and move unused alternatives out of the way until you are ready to compare them.

3. Remove obvious clutter regularly

Sell or move low-value items before they turn every inventory screen into a search problem.

4. Distribute items by role

Put useful items on characters who are most likely to use them so access during combat is faster.
