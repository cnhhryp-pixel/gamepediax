Valheim New Biome First Trip Guide

A Valheim first-trip guide for entering a new biome with better food, repaired gear, a fallback point and a reconnaissance mindset.

The first trip into a new biome should teach you the terrain and threats, not carry every valuable item you own.

Use stronger food than for routine chores

A new biome is a poor place to discover that your normal food set is the limiting factor.

Repair before leaving

Avoid adding equipment durability to the list of unknowns.

Create a recovery plan

A nearby fallback point can turn a death from a major setback into a short recovery.

Treat the first trip as reconnaissance

Learn threats, routes and resources before trying to maximize the haul.
