Best Party Balance in Baldur's Gate 3: How to Build a Flexible Group

A Baldur's Gate 3 party-balance guide focused on frontline pressure, ranged options, support, control and exploration utility.

The best party is not one fixed composition. A flexible group covers several kinds of problems without making every character do the same job.

Cover frontline pressure

At least one character should be comfortable occupying dangerous space or protecting more fragile allies.

Keep dependable ranged options

Ranged damage helps the party contribute when terrain or positioning limits melee access.

Include recovery and control

Healing, buffs, control and utility can prevent difficult encounters from becoming pure damage races.

Keep exploration skills in mind

A party that handles combat well but struggles with the rest of the game can still feel inefficient.
