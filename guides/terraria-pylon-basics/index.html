Terraria Pylon Basics: Planning a Faster World

A Terraria pylon basics guide focused on planning travel hubs, reducing repeated movement and avoiding overbuilding early.

Pylons are most useful when they reduce the trips you repeat most often. Plan around travel convenience instead of trying to build a perfect network immediately.

Think in travel hubs

Choose locations that meaningfully shorten routes to areas you visit repeatedly.

Keep each hub easy to use

Travel points should connect naturally to housing, storage or the region they serve.

Build the network gradually

Add new hubs when they solve a real travel problem instead of filling the map for its own sake.

Revisit the network as progression changes

The most useful travel destinations can change as new areas become important.
