Stardew Valley Mining Checklist for Early Progression

A Stardew Valley mining checklist for early progression covering food, inventory space, tool readiness and choosing a clear objective before entering the mines.

Mining trips are more productive when you prepare for one goal instead of trying to collect everything. A small checklist reduces wasted energy and repeated trips.

1. Prepare before leaving the farm

Bring enough food for the trip, clear inventory space and make sure your tool is ready for the amount of mining you plan to do.

2. Decide what the trip is for

Choose whether the main objective is depth, ore, stone or general resource collection. That decision changes what you keep and when you return.

3. Protect inventory space

Avoid carrying unrelated items into the mines. Empty slots make it easier to stay longer without throwing away useful resources.

4. Return before the trip becomes inefficient

If energy, food or inventory space is almost gone, leave while the trip is still productive and convert the resources into upgrades.
