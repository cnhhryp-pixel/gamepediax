Elden Ring Weapon Class Guide: How to Pick a Style

A spoiler-light Elden Ring weapon class guide for choosing a style by speed, range, commitment and the way you prefer to control fights.

Weapon classes feel different because they ask different questions about timing and spacing. Pick the style that makes your decisions clearer, not just the one with the biggest number.

Think about attack speed and commitment

Faster styles can recover quickly, while slower attacks may reward better timing and spacing.

Consider how much reach you want

Extra reach can make spacing more forgiving, while shorter-range weapons may reward closer pressure.

Test the moveset before investing heavily

Use a weapon class long enough to understand its rhythm before spending scarce upgrade materials.

Let the class shape the build direction

Once a style feels right, reinforce it with coherent stats, survivability and supporting equipment.
