Best Early Upgrades in Subnautica: How to Prioritize

A Subnautica early upgrades guide for deciding whether oxygen, mobility, storage or vehicle support should come first.

The best early upgrade is usually the one that removes the problem ending your trips most often.

Prioritize oxygen when dives end too early

More breathing margin can improve exploration and gathering at the same time.

Prioritize mobility when travel consumes the session

Faster movement creates more useful time for exploration.

Prioritize storage when every trip ends in clutter

More organized capacity can improve the value of every resource run.

Prioritize vehicle support when range becomes the bottleneck

Once the surrounding area is familiar, stronger travel options can open the next layer of progression.

Upgrade the bottleneck, not the category

Do not choose upgrades by a fixed universal order. Think about what is ending productive trips right now. If oxygen is fine but travel is slow, another oxygen improvement may create little value. If storage is chaotic, a mobility upgrade can make the chaos arrive faster without solving it.

Subnautica early upgrade FAQ

What should I upgrade first?

Upgrade the system that most often ends or interrupts your trips: oxygen, mobility, storage or exploration range.

When should vehicle support become a priority?

When the nearby area is familiar and the main limitation is how far or how safely you can travel beyond it.

Can storage really be a progression upgrade?

Yes. Better storage shortens unloading, reduces duplicate gathering and helps you see which materials are actually missing.
