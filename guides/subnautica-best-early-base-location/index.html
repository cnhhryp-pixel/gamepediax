Best Early Base Location in Subnautica: What to Look For

A practical Subnautica early base location guide covering safety, resource access, travel distance and future expansion.

A useful early base location reduces repeated travel and inventory pressure. It should be easy to recognize, easy to return to and close enough to resources you use often.

Choose a location you can use repeatedly without stress

A base is more useful when the route home is predictable and not unnecessarily dangerous.

Consider recurring resource trips

Being closer to materials you need often can save more time than choosing a visually perfect spot.

Think about where exploration is moving next

A location that supports current and near-future routes can stay useful longer.

Leave enough space for useful additions

You do not need a giant base, but avoid trapping future storage or vehicle support.
