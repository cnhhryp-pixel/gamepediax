Elden Ring Open World Route Planning Guide

A spoiler-light Elden Ring route planning guide for exploration, map marking and deciding when to leave an area and return later.

Route planning in an open world is about keeping options visible. You do not need to clear every location on the first pass.

Mark unresolved locations

Save places you want to revisit so changing direction does not mean losing track of progress.

Use detours to build strength

Side exploration can improve equipment and resources without forcing a difficult encounter.

Leave when progress stalls

If attempts are repeating without useful learning, another route may be more productive.

Return after meaningful upgrades

Old obstacles are useful benchmarks for testing whether your build and understanding have improved.
