Best Early Upgrades in No Man's Sky: What to Prioritize

A practical No Man's Sky early-upgrade guide for deciding between inventory, travel, hazard protection and scanning improvements.

The best early upgrade is usually the one that removes the problem interrupting your exploration most often.

Prioritize inventory when space ends trips early

More usable capacity can improve gathering, trading and exploration at the same time.

Prioritize travel when distance is the bottleneck

Mobility upgrades create more useful time and make more destinations practical.

Prioritize protection when environments stop progress

Hazard support is valuable when it keeps you productive in places you already want to visit.

Prioritize scanning when information saves time

Better information can reduce random searching and make resource runs more targeted.

Use a bottleneck sequence instead of a fixed upgrade order

Upgrade the system that most often interrupts the activity you want to do next. After that upgrade, reassess. The highest-value second upgrade may be completely different because the first bottleneck is gone.

No Man's Sky early upgrade FAQ

Is inventory always the best first upgrade?

No. Inventory is excellent when capacity interrupts several activities, but travel or hazard protection can be more valuable if they are the real blocker.

How do I know an upgrade was worth it?

The problem it targeted should stop ending or shortening your sessions as often.

Should I spread resources across several upgrade categories?

Usually not early. Solving one clear bottleneck completely can create more value than several partial improvements.
