Baldur's Gate 3 Combat Preparation Checklist

A Baldur's Gate 3 combat preparation checklist covering party positioning, resources, consumables and environmental advantages.

Many difficult fights become easier before the first attack happens. Positioning, resources and environmental awareness can matter as much as character builds.

1. Look at the battlefield first

Check elevation, chokepoints, hazards and routes before committing the party.

2. Know which limited resources are available

Review spell slots, abilities and consumables so you do not discover too late that the party is already depleted.

3. Put each character where their role works

Frontline characters, ranged attackers and support characters should start where they can contribute quickly.

4. Change positioning before rebuilding characters

If a fight repeatedly fails, try a different approach to space and target priority before assuming the whole build is wrong.
