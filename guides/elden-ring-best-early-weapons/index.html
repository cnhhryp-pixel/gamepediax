Best Early Weapons in Elden Ring: How to Choose

A spoiler-light Elden Ring early weapons guide for choosing a weapon by moveset, requirements, upgrade cost and build fit.

The best early weapon is the one that fits your play style and can be supported by your current stats and upgrade resources. Comfort and consistency matter more than chasing a single universal answer.

Start with a moveset you understand

A familiar moveset makes learning enemy timing and spacing much easier.

Check practical stat requirements

A weapon is only useful if your build can support it without sacrificing everything else.

Consider upgrade commitment

If two weapons feel similar, the one you can upgrade consistently may be the better early choice.

Choose the weapon that fits the build direction

The best option is the one that reinforces the style you already enjoy rather than forcing a complete rebuild.

Build a short list instead of chasing one universal best

Test a few weapons that fit your preferred range and attack rhythm. Use them long enough to judge recovery time, reach and stamina pressure. Then commit upgrade materials to the option that feels reliable across ordinary enemies and harder fights. The strongest early choice is usually the weapon you can use consistently, not the one that only looks best on paper.

Early weapon FAQ

Should I switch weapons every time I find a higher-damage option?

No. A new weapon should earn its place by improving your actual combat loop, not just one displayed number.

When should I commit upgrade materials?

Commit after a weapon has proven comfortable and useful across several encounters, not immediately after picking it up.

What if I am unsure about my final build?

Keep the early setup simple. A coherent temporary build is better than spreading stats and upgrades across several possible futures.
