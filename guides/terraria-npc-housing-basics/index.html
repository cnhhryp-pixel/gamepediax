Terraria NPC Housing Basics

A Terraria NPC housing basics guide focused on readable layouts, easy access and avoiding an overbuilt early settlement.

Early NPC housing works best when it is simple, easy to reach and easy to expand. You can optimize aesthetics and specialized layouts later.

Start with simple repeatable rooms

A consistent layout makes expansion faster and reduces the chance of forgetting basic housing needs.

Keep housing easy to reach

NPC areas should not require awkward travel every time you need a service.

Leave room for future additions

A compact settlement is useful, but avoid trapping yourself in a layout that is hard to extend.

Group related services when useful

Organizing NPCs around the way you use the base can reduce repeated movement.
