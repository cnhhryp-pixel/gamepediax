Cyberpunk 2077 Mission Preparation Checklist

A Cyberpunk 2077 mission preparation checklist covering loadout, healing, cyberware synergy and inventory space.

Mission preparation does not need to be complicated. A quick check of the systems you rely on most can prevent avoidable friction once the job begins.

1. Check the primary weapon setup

Make sure the weapons you intend to use fit the build and are easy to access.

2. Check healing and recovery options

Do not discover during a difficult encounter that your recovery supplies or defensive options are insufficient.

3. Review cyberware around the current play style

Prioritize pieces that work together rather than mixing several unrelated ideas.

4. Leave inventory space for rewards

A clean inventory makes post-mission comparison easier and reduces time spent sorting in the middle of progression.
