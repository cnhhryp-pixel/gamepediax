Cyberpunk 2077 Mission Loadout Checklist

A Cyberpunk 2077 mission loadout checklist for weapons, healing, cyberware synergy, backup options and inventory space.

A quick loadout check before a mission can prevent avoidable friction without turning preparation into a complicated routine.

Check the primary weapon setup

Use weapons that fit the build and are easy to access.

Check recovery options

Make sure healing and defensive tools are available before entering a difficult job.

Keep one backup option

A secondary tool can help when the main style is awkward for a specific encounter.

Leave room for rewards

A clean inventory makes post-mission comparison much easier.

Use a two-minute pre-mission check

Before starting a harder job, confirm the primary weapon, recovery option, one backup tool and enough inventory space. Avoid rebuilding the entire character for every mission. The goal is to remove obvious friction while keeping the combat loop familiar.

Cyberpunk 2077 mission loadout FAQ

Do I need a different loadout for every mission?

No. Keep the core build stable and adjust only when a mission exposes a specific weakness.

What makes a good backup option?

Something that covers a problem the main style handles poorly, such as range, pressure or positioning.

Why clean inventory before a mission?

It makes new rewards easier to compare and prevents clutter from interrupting the next decision.
