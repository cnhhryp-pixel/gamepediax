Cyberpunk 2077 Mission Loadout Checklist

A Cyberpunk 2077 mission loadout checklist for weapons, healing, cyberware synergy, backup options and inventory space.

A quick loadout check before a mission can prevent avoidable friction without turning preparation into a complicated routine.

Check the primary weapon setup

Use weapons that fit the build and are easy to access.

Check recovery options

Make sure healing and defensive tools are available before entering a difficult job.

Keep one backup option

A secondary tool can help when the main style is awkward for a specific encounter.

Leave room for rewards

A clean inventory makes post-mission comparison much easier.
