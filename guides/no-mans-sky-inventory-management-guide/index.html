No Man's Sky Inventory Management Guide

A practical No Man's Sky inventory management guide for keeping travel resources, upgrades and collected materials under control.

Inventory space is a progression resource. The cleaner your loadout, the easier it is to explore, gather and respond to unexpected opportunities.

1. Reserve space for core travel resources

Keep essential survival and travel materials available instead of letting low-priority items crowd them out.

2. Remove items without a near-term purpose

Sell, refine or store materials that are only creating clutter. Hoarding everything slows every later decision.

3. Group resources by use

Keep related materials together so you can quickly see what is abundant and what is actually missing.

4. Review inventory after major trips

Do not let expedition clutter become permanent storage. A clean reset keeps the next session efficient.
