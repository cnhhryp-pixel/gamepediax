No Man's Sky Inventory Upgrade Priority Guide

A No Man's Sky inventory upgrade guide for deciding when more capacity is more valuable than travel, hazard or equipment upgrades.

Inventory capacity is most valuable when space pressure interrupts several parts of the game at once.

Measure how often space ends a trip early

If gathering, trading and exploration all stop because of capacity, inventory may be the highest-value upgrade.

Protect space for core resources

Extra capacity matters most when it lets travel essentials and useful discoveries coexist.

Compare capacity with other bottlenecks

If travel or hazard exposure is the real problem, inventory may not be the first upgrade to solve.

Keep the larger inventory organized

More slots only help if clutter does not expand to fill them immediately.
