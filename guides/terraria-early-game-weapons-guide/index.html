Terraria Early Game Weapons Guide: Choosing a Weapon Type

A Terraria early-game weapons guide for choosing between weapon styles based on range, movement and how you prefer to fight.

The best early weapon is not automatically the one with the highest number. A weapon that fits your movement and targeting habits can create more consistent damage.

Decide how much range you want

Longer range can make learning enemy patterns easier, while close-range styles may reward better positioning.

Match the weapon to your movement

Choose a style that lets you keep attacking without constantly fighting your own movement limitations.

Consider how easy the weapon is to support

A strong weapon can be awkward if its resource or setup requirements interrupt progression.

Upgrade after the style proves useful

Test the weapon type first, then invest more heavily once it consistently fits your play.
